# CLAUDE.md — Elke Dag Zin

A Flemish (Belgian Dutch) learning app for one learner (Ari, Antwerp, A2 → C1). It is hosted on **GitHub Pages** as a full-screen Home Screen app for iPhone. Conversation practice happens in a **Claude Project** (Elke Dag Zin · Praten) and comes back into the app as a pasted report. Read `SPEC-2.0.md` first, then `FORMATS.md` and `REFERENCE.md`.

## How the code is built
- Source parts live in `src/`: `content*.js` (curriculum data), `app*.js` (engine and views), `style.css`, `sw.js` (service worker template).
  - 2.0 additions: `app14.js` (service worker registration, Praten start block, report import, EDZ-WOORDEN word-set import with the daily release of new words, Documenten voor Claude, first-run migration), `content6.js` (the measurable `doel` per scene) and `content7.js` (gap words, the Algemeen words with `u: 0`, extra parts of speech). 2.1: `app15.js` (the Woorden tab with *Bekijk per* Soort · Stad · Thema · Set, set pages with rename and *Deel als woordenlijst*, word themes, *Importeer woordenlijst* under Ik) and `content8.js` (the 16 themes and a theme per lemma; every app word must have one, `tests/report.js` checks it). 2.2: `app16.js` (conjugation inside a sentence, synonym/antonym and definition cards, synonyms and antonyms on the word card, the clearer *Verbeter de zin* card) and `content9.js` (synonyms and antonyms per lemma, Belgian Dutch, only where a good one exists). 2.3: the maps in `app8.js` are built from squares and roads (`roadBetween`: line, L or curve) instead of one curve through every town; `flows.js` checks crossings and squares. 2.4: `app17.js` (progress on the maps: houses, flags, Ronde flags, bunting, gold; the town's goal card; one opdracht per town in `S.missions`). 2.5: `app18.js` (the card label pill, the English cue on fill-in cards, the Scroll listening card with a question) and five comprehension questions per text in `content5.js`. 2.6: `app19.js` and `src/woordenboek/*.jsonl` (the built-in dictionary: build.py writes `woordenboek.json`; the page loads it after start, the service worker keeps it offline; tapping a word without a card opens its filled-in card, one tap adds it; the Nieuw woord form and the Woorden search use it; format and rules in `src/woordenboek/README.md`). `app1.js` holds the local Store, `app5.js` the backup.
  - The Grote Markt is always the last square of a town, so its id follows the squares (u12q5 after u12q4 was added). Only ever add squares to unbuilt towns.
- `python3 build.py` concatenates them in a fixed order into **`index.html`** at the repo root, the file Pages serves, and writes `manifest.webmanifest` and `sw.js`. Keep it one self-contained page, plus those two files and the icons.
  - The service worker cache name is a hash of the page, so every release bumps it by itself.
  - The icons (`icon180.png`, `icon192.png`, `icon512.png`, `icon512-maskable.png`) are committed. Delete one to render it again; that needs the Python `playwright` package.
- `praten-project/*.md` are the Claude Project's files. `Curriculum.md`, `Woorden.md` and `Scenes.md` are generated by the app (Ik → Documenten voor Claude); when content changes, regenerate them so `tests/flows.js` stays green. The repo's `Woorden.md` also lists Ari's imported set *Mijn woorden 1*; `tests/report.js` checks that part after importing `tests/reports/mijn-woorden-1.txt`.
- **No frameworks, no bundler, no runtime dependencies.** Plain JS, functions declared at top level. A later part may redefine an earlier function on purpose (last declaration wins); note it in a comment when you do.
- Content ids (`u7`, `u7q2`, `u7s14`, word ids) **never change**: saved progress refers to them.

## The three card modes
- **Herhalen** tests: the review schedule decides, you type, order and fix. Every answer counts.
- **Scroll** practises with one tap, never typing: multiple choice, de/het, word cards, synonym/antonym and definition cards, conjugation in a sentence. Synonym, antonym and definition cards stay in Scroll only.
- **Kaarten** studies a group Ari picks (a set, theme, town or part of speech): flip, then knew it / not yet.
- All three share the word card, the feedback (right/wrong, the answer, why) and the colours.
- Every card names its kind in one label pill at the top (`kindLabel`), e.g. *⇄ Tegenovergestelde*. A multiple-choice card always has at least three choices, and exactly one of them fits.

## Progress on the maps (one language on every level)
- A building is a piece of work: **grey** until done, **in colour with a yellow flag** when gehaald (80%+), a **flag with a white star** at 100% (Ontdek and Gebruik count as 100% when done; a test-out is gehaald).
- Stad map: each plein has its own five guild houses in the plein's colours and order. Pad: each town has three plein houses and a town hall for its Grote Markt.
- **Ronde 2**: colourful flags across the plein (stad map) and across its guild houses (plein). **Ronde 3**: the same flags in gold, with sparkles.
- **Bunting** over a Grote Markt (and around a town on the Pad): the Grote Markt is gehaald. **Gold kerb + sparkles**: perfect (plein: all five at 100% and Ronde 3; town: all pleinen perfect, Grote Markt 100%, opdracht done).
- The explanation lives in Ik → Hoe werkt Elke Dag Zin?, not on the maps.

## Standing rules (from Ari, apply to every change)
1. **Locking:** squares and towns open in order. A locked square or town offers a **test-out**. **Testmodus** (switch in Ik) opens everything for testing.
2. **Flashcards are welcome but never the default.** The default practice is sentences, in context.
3. **Interface labels are Dutch**; long-press shows English (`data-en`).
4. **Colour carries meaning, white UI:**
   - green = tappable (filled green = the next step)
   - blue = *de*, red = *het*
   - gold = the verb (slot 2 and the verb end)
   - **underline = tap it** (word card, or add the word to a list), nothing else. The subject is **bold**; never use `<u>` for grammar.
5. **Dutch-first definitions**; every noun shows *de/het*; verbs have full conjugation overviews.
6. **The app teaches itself:** first-run tour (only on first use, with a switch to show it again), one-time tips per screen (with a switch), and a short "Zo werkt het" on every exercise type.
7. **Core unit** = one grammar point × one Antwerp theme × one can-do. Every square has the same five guild houses, which are five different skills: Ontdek, Oefen, Lees, Luister, Gebruik.
8. Standard **Belgian Dutch** norms in all content.
9. Map style: retro pixel world, cyclist protagonist, natural features, not overcrowded. Roads mix straight stretches, 90° turns and curves; they never cross, never run over a square, and enter a square at the middle of a side like a street at an intersection. Roads and squares share one stone. The cyclist always faces the way he rides. Names on the maps stay at their own square (they may stand on the square itself) and never cover a house, its flag, a road, the cyclist, the Ronde flags or bunting, or another name. The next-step arrow bobs on top of the name. `placeSign` in `app8.js` takes the free spot nearest the square's centre; `tests/validate.js` checks the Pad and every town map (no overlap, at most 16 px from the square's edge).

## Writing content for a new town or plein (Ari's review, 2.5)
`tests/validate.js` checks each of these for every built town, so a new plein fails the tests until it follows them:
- **Lees:** every plein has a text in `READ` with **five** questions about what the text says (who, what, where, when, why, how much). Each has three different choices, and the right answer moves around. Never ask about grammar or word order ("In … staat het werkwoord…", "de of het?", "is een bevel"): the other guild houses test that. 4 of 5 is gehaald, 5 of 5 is 100%.
- **Vertaal:** a sentence whose slot 1 is not the subject (inversie) says what kind of thing comes first, never the words themselves: *Begin met de tijd / de plaats / de bijzin / het voorwerp (inversie)* (`voorveldKind` in `app2.js`; odd cases go in `VV_KIND`). It accepts only that order. Subject-first sentences accept every correct order.
- **Kies het woord / Vervoeg in de zin:** the English sentence marks the word that belongs in the gap (`enCue`). When a sentence uses a different English word than the gloss (gloss *bicycle*, sentence *bike*), add it to `EN_ALSO` in `app18.js`.
- **Choices:** at least three on every card, also for Ari's own words with a rare part of speech (an own pronoun like *iemand*). The test imports *Mijn woorden 1* to check this.
- **Order of the guild houses:** Ontdek → Oefen → Lees → Luister → Gebruik. `nextStep` and the cyclist follow it.
- **Every word in the app's texts has a card:** an app word, or a woordenboek word. A new text with a word that has neither fails the tests; add the word to `src/woordenboek/` (names go in `WB_NAMES`).
- **Speaker buttons** use `speakBtn` (or `SPEAK_ICON`): they turn into a stop button while speaking.

## Before you commit
- The tests use Playwright for Node (`npm install` installs it as a dev dependency) and serve the repo over http, so the service worker runs. Set `SHOTS=some/dir` to save screenshots.
- `node tests/validate.js`: every generated exercise must accept its own answer (currently 6,354 checks, 0 failures). Every reorder variant must be valid Dutch. It also checks the content rules above.
- `node tests/flows.js`: Playwright run through welcome → tour → Pad → square → session → Lees (five questions, the stop button, Lees after Oefen) → Scroll Luister and Vertaal with inversie → Grote Markt → Woorden/Kaarten → Naslag search → Documenten voor Claude → backup/restore (also a 1.5 backup, also on the first-run screen) → progress on the stad map, plein and Pad, the opdracht box → manifest, icons, offline, update toast, installability, safe areas. It fails on any page error.
- `node tests/report.js`: the report parser on the fixtures in `tests/reports/`. These cover a valid report, smart quotes, extra text around the block, a missing end marker, unknown square ids, words already known, code fences, dagboek and foto reports, no report at all, and invalid JSON (must show a friendly error, never crash). It also imports the FORMATS.md example through the review screen and checks `EDZ-START v1` for all four modes. Then the word lists: moved and gap words, the new squares, and `EDZ-WOORDEN v1` (preview counts, import, pasting twice, broken entries, the daily release).
- Look at the screens you changed at 390×844 (iPhone). Check the safe areas at top and bottom.
- Rebuild `index.html` and commit it together with `src/`.

## Things to avoid
- No `window.claude` calls. Version 2.0 has no Claude inside the page; Claude is in the Project.
- Don't put API keys anywhere in the repo.
- Don't let a content edit change existing ids. Add new items with new ids.
- Don't make the Scroll feed, flashcards or the map slow down opening the app.
