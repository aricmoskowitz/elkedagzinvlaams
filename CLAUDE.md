# CLAUDE.md — Elke Dag Zin

A Flemish (Belgian Dutch) learning app for one learner (Antwerp, A2 → C1). It is hosted on **GitHub Pages** as a full-screen Home Screen app for iPhone. Conversation practice happens in a **Claude Project** (Elke Dag Zin · Praten) and comes back into the app as a pasted report. Read `SPEC-2.0.md` first, then `FORMATS.md` and `REFERENCE.md`.

## The three card modes
- **Herhalen** tests: the review schedule decides, you type, order and fix. Every answer counts.
- **Scroll** practises with one tap, never typing: multiple choice, de/het, word cards, synonym/antonym and definition cards, conjugation in a sentence. Synonym, antonym and definition cards stay in Scroll only.
  - 2.11: on a *Ken je dit woord?* card, Wist ik / Nog niet (a tap or a swipe right / left) sends the card sideways and the next one scrolls in (`feedKnew`, `feedSwipe` in `app5.js`). A Scroll card never grows past its own screen. Verbs have no ge / gij forms (not in the overview, not in the data).
- **Kaarten** studies a group the user picks (a set, theme, town or part of speech): flip, then knew it / not yet. 2.16: what you know is kept in four parts per word (`S.kaart[id]` = `{sn, se, fn, fe}`: eenvoudig or volledig × Dutch or English in front; 1 wist ik, 0 nog niet). Eenvoudig = the word itself; volledig = every form too (a noun's article, plural and diminutive; a verb's conjugations; an adjective's forms). A noun always shows its article on the card (*het huis*), also in front. The card itself stays clean: the four parts show in the word lists of the Woorden tab (`kaartMini`, a 2 × 2 grid: rows eenvoudig / volledig, columns NL → EN / EN → NL, with a legend above the list). *↶ Vorige kaart* (or Backspace) undoes the last card: its status, the review item and the count. *Sla over wat ik al ken* skips words already known for the chosen kind and side.
- All three share the word card, the feedback (right/wrong, the answer, why) and the colours.
- Every card names its kind in one label pill at the top (`kindLabel`), e.g. *⇄ Tegenovergestelde*. A multiple-choice card always has at least three choices, and exactly one of them fits.

## Progress on the maps (one language on every level)
- A building is a piece of work: **grey** until done, **in colour with a yellow flag** when gehaald (80%+), a **flag with a white star** at 100% (Ontdek and Gebruik count as 100% when done; a test-out is gehaald for Ontdek and Oefen).
- Stad map: each plein has its own five guild houses in the plein's colours and order. Pad: each town has three plein houses and a town hall for its Grote Markt. 2.14: on the Pad these four buildings are drawn 1.4× (`PAD_K`) around their ground line and never overlap (`worldRoles` picks spots whose boxes, flag included, don't touch; a building without room shrinks to 1.2× or 1×; besides the old spots each town has extra 2×2 spots on free grass, marked as a house only when used; a spot on the top rows is used last); Wegwijzer stands two tiles lower for room; at most two small grey filler houses per town, where they touch nothing; fewer trees. The Speelfort is 7×7 tiles. A plein house gets the yellow flag when 4 of its 5 guild houses are gehaald or better, the star flag when 4 are at 100% (`padFlag`), small colourful flags over it after Ronde 2, and turns gold after Ronde 3. One banner per square: on the Pad the town square gets one string of bunting across it when every plein has passed Ronde 2 and the Grote Markt is gehaald (gold when every plein passed Ronde 3). The Speelfort stands in the top-right corner of the Pad with its name right below it.
- **Ronde 2**: colourful flags across the plein (stad map) and across its guild houses (plein). **Ronde 3**: the same flags in gold, with sparkles.
- **Bunting** over a Grote Markt: the Grote Markt is gehaald (on the Pad, across the town's square, once every plein has passed Ronde 2 too). **Theme sign** (a tram, frites…) in the middle of every square: gold on a plein that passed Ronde 3; on the Pad and on the grass of the town map when every plein of the town has. **Gold kerb + sparkles**: perfect (plein: all five at 100% and Ronde 3; town: all pleinen perfect, Grote Markt 100%, opdracht done).
- 2.18: on the plein screen an Oefen or Gebruik guild house that is not gehaald yet shows *· beste poging 44% · 80% nodig* next to its name, where a gehaald house shows its % (`bestTry` in `app8.js`: Oefen from `S.sq[q].tried.oefen`, the best session score also below 80%; Gebruik from the best report score in either kanaal, `best.spreken` / `best.schrijven`). The house stays grey until 80%.

## Standing rules (from the user, apply to every change)
1. **Locking:** squares and towns open in order. A locked square or town offers a **test-out**: 10 questions for a plein, 20 for a stad, 90% to pass (`TESTOUT_N`, `TESTOUT_PASS` in `app3.js`). A pass marks only Ontdek and Oefen (yellow flag, no star) and sets `S.sq[q].test`; Luister, Lees and Gebruik stay to do. `sqPassed` (complete or tested) opens the next plein, the Grote Markt and the next town; `sqComplete` (klaar) still needs Luister. **Testmodus** (switch in Ik) opens everything for testing.
2. **Flashcards are welcome but never the default.** The default practice is sentences, in context.
3. **Interface labels are Dutch**; long-press shows English (`data-en`).
4. **Colour carries meaning, white UI:**
   - green = tappable (filled green = the next step)
   - blue = *de*, red = *het*
   - gold = the verb (slot 2 and the verb end)
   - **underline = tap it** (word card, or add the word to a list), nothing else. The subject is **bold**; never use `<u>` for grammar.
5. **Dutch-first definitions**; every noun shows *de/het*; verbs have full conjugation overviews.
6. **The app teaches itself:** first-run tour (only on first use, with a switch to show it again), one-time tips per screen (with a switch), and a short "Zo werkt het" on every exercise type.
7. **Core unit** = one grammar point × one Antwerp theme × one can-do. Every square has the same five guild houses, which are five different skills: Ontdek, Oefen, Lees, Luister, Gebruik.
8. Standard **Belgian Dutch** norms in all content.
9. Map style: retro pixel world, cyclist protagonist, natural features, not overcrowded. Roads mix straight stretches, 90° turns and curves; they never cross, never run over a square, and enter a square at the middle of a side like a street at an intersection. Roads and squares share one stone. The cyclist always faces the way he rides. Names on the maps stay at their own square (they may stand on the square itself) and never cover a house, its flag, a road, the cyclist, the Ronde flags or bunting, or another name. The next-step arrow bobs on top of the name; only the next step has one (green), open towns and pleinen have none. `placeSign` in `app8.js` takes the free spot nearest the square's centre; `tests/validate.js` checks the Pad and every town map (no overlap, at most 16 px from the square's edge).

## Writing content for a new town or plein (the user's review, 2.5)
`tests/validate.js` checks each of these for every built town, so a new plein fails the tests until it follows them:
- **Lees:** every plein has a text in `READ` with **five** questions about what the text says (who, what, where, when, why, how much). Each has three different choices, and the right answer moves around. Never ask about grammar or word order ("In … staat het werkwoord…", "de of het?", "is een bevel"): the other guild houses test that. 4 of 5 is gehaald, 5 of 5 is 100%.
- **Vertaal:** a sentence whose slot 1 is not the subject (inversie) says what kind of thing comes first, never the words themselves: *Begin met de tijd / de plaats / de bijzin / het voorwerp (inversie)* (`voorveldKind` in `app2.js`; odd cases go in `VV_KIND`). It accepts only that order. Subject-first sentences accept every correct order. **Zet in de juiste volgorde** accepts every correct order (`orderVariants`); when the learner's order is correct but differs from the model, the feedback shows their own sentence and frame, with the model as *Ook juist* (`ownOrder`).
- **Kies het woord / Vervoeg in de zin:** the English sentence marks the word that belongs in the gap (`enCue`). When a sentence uses a different English word than the gloss (gloss *bicycle*, sentence *bike*), add it to `EN_ALSO` in `app18.js`.
- **Choices:** at least three on every card, also for the user's own words with a rare part of speech (an own pronoun like *iemand*). The test imports *Mijn woorden 1* to check this.
- **Order of the guild houses:** Ontdek → Oefen → Lees → Luister → Gebruik. `nextStep` and the cyclist follow it.
- **Every word in the app's texts has a card:** an app word, or a woordenboek word. A new text with a word that has neither fails the tests; add the word to `src/woordenboek/` (names go in `WB_NAMES`).
- **Theme sign:** every town has one in `TOWN_LANDMARK` (`app21.js`), a simple 14 px wide pixel sprite for its theme (no stands, tracks or steam). `tests/validate.js` checks it stays inside each square and off the waiting cyclist, that names keep off it, and that the copies on the grass stand on free ground.
- **Gebruik scenes:** every plein and the Grote Markt need their own scene in `content10.js` (title, role, roleEn, goal, goalEn, open, doel). The doel parts are split on " en " (not inside brackets), so write "plus" when a part needs "and". The Grote Markt doel combines the pleinen (at least 4 parts). `tests/validate.js` checks it; regenerate `praten-project/Scenes.md`.
- **Speaker buttons** use `speakBtn` (or `SPEAK_ICON`): they turn into a stop button while speaking.

## Before you commit
- The tests use Playwright for Node (`npm install` installs it as a dev dependency) and serve the repo over http, so the service worker runs. Set `SHOTS=some/dir` to save screenshots.
- `node tests/validate.js`: every generated exercise must accept its own answer (currently 8,489 checks, 0 failures, including the 2,000 woordenboek entries). Every reorder variant must be valid Dutch. It also checks the content rules above.
- `node tests/flows.js`: Playwright run through welcome → tour → Pad → square → session → Lees (five questions, the stop button, Lees after Oefen) → the woordenboek (tap an unknown word, one-tap add, the Nieuw woord form fills itself in, offline) → Scroll Luister and Vertaal with inversie → Grote Markt → Woorden/Kaarten → Naslag search → Documenten voor Claude → backup/restore (also a 1.5 backup, also on the first-run screen) → progress on the stad map, plein and Pad, the opdracht box → manifest, icons, offline, update toast, installability, safe areas. It fails on any page error.
- `node tests/report.js`: the report parser on the fixtures in `tests/reports/`. These cover a valid report, smart quotes, extra text around the block, a missing end marker, unknown square ids, words already known, code fences, dagboek and foto reports, no report at all, and invalid JSON (must show a friendly error, never crash). It also imports the FORMATS.md example through the review screen and checks `EDZ-START v1` for all four modes. Then the word lists: moved and gap words, the new squares, and `EDZ-WOORDEN v1` (preview counts, import, pasting twice, broken entries, the daily release). Then brief 2 with the five `thema-*.txt` fixtures (678 words): 0 new · 150 existing · 150 aangevuld, idempotent, every word themed, an existing theme kept, a report's `thema`, `Woorden.md` (Woordenboek section, no duplicates, themes in sets, deleted sets gone), the backup's *klaar*, and deleting a mistake.
- Look at the screens you changed at 390×844 (iPhone). Check the safe areas at top and bottom.
- Rebuild `index.html` and commit it together with `src/`.

## Things to avoid
- No `window.claude` calls. Version 2.0 has no Claude inside the page; Claude is in the Project.
- Don't put API keys anywhere in the repo.
- Don't let a content edit change existing ids. Add new items with new ids.
- Don't make the Scroll feed, flashcards or the map slow down opening the app.
